Training tools

Seven tools

Calculators and references for lifting and eating. Free, no sign-up.

  • Exercise glossary

    Demo photos and numbered instructions for the exercises in the programs. Search by name, equipment or body part.

  • TDEE calculator

    Daily calories to maintain, cut or gain, from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, with a protein range.

  • Macro calculator

    Calories, protein, carbs and fat in grams for fat loss, maintenance or muscle gain.

  • One rep max calculator

    Estimate a 1RM from a weight and a rep count, with a table of training percentages.

  • Plate calculator

    What plates to load on each side of the bar, in kg or lb, from the plates your gym has.

  • RPE and RIR chart

    Convert RPE to reps in reserve, with a description of each level from RPE 6 to 10.

  • Programs by training days

    Pick the days a week you train and see the programs built for that count.

40+ pre-written programs. The quiz matches you to one.
